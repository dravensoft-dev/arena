# Colour slots, part by part

> **The slot half of the design specification.** [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md) beside it states what
> `color-slots.json` is, how a slot is named and what `check:color-slots` holds. This page states how
> each part of the `choices` and `fields` groups sits, fades and hovers, and the shape of a member
> part that reads a group.

## The choices and fields groups

`choices` does with no options: box, mark, knob, segment and track. A part with `on` sits its fill on that sibling's fill and its glyph on its own fill when it paints one, and every pair of it is a mark at 3:1 (D69); a part whose states hold no rest, as `choices.mark`, answers `selected` alone (D70). An edge slot shows only where its member paints a border: `choices-box-edge-*` on Checkbox.box and Radio.ring (Switch.track, Slider.rail and Slider.fill read it and draw no border), `choices-knob-edge-*` on Slider.thumb. An answered `disabled` or `selected-disabled` slot stops the fade on its own part, which carries it as `opacity-[var(--<stem>-fade-<state>,var(--fade-disabled))]`. A part drawn inside a faded part derives no disabled state and fades with its parent (D72): Switch.knob, Switch.icon and Slider.thumb (`choices.knob`, rest and selected) and Checkbox.check and Radio.dot (`choices.mark`, selected alone).
`fields` does with options on `field` (neutral, error, valid and readonly; `field` paints fill and edge, and readonly answers its fill alone, with no hover, through the option's own `properties` and `states`), `status` and `counter`, and none on `value`, `required`, `error` and `stepper`. The error and valid options declare `halo`, and a member's ring reads `slots.ts:haloValue(catalogue, member, option)`, the option's `edge-rest` chain at that level, so answering `fields-error-field-edge-rest` moves the border and the halo together. A field's `hover` is derived from its rest and its fill takes the group exception `fields-<option>-field-fill-hover` (D19) for neutral, error and valid; every field's text reads `fields.value` (D74), as `inkFrom` says for Select, Textarea, PinInput and ConfirmDialog.input; `ArenaFileInput.zone` reads its hover inside the `dragging` branch (`hoverBy`, D68). `fields.value` sits inside its field and derives no disabled state (D72), and a placeholder reads its ink chain at `level-ink-muted` with no slot of its own (R8). An answered `…-field-…-disabled` slot stops the fade on its field the way a choices slot does: the root of Input, NumberField, Combobox, Select, PinInput, Textarea and FileInput carries no fade, and each part under it carries its own (Select's wrap carries the fade of the select, its caret and its glyph). A disabled compound of a field that is no native control repeats its reads under `hover:`, so a hovered disabled field keeps an answer; `:where()` gives a compound no specificity, so compounds run in manifest order, rest before drag before disabled.

A group part with `over` sits every slot on its sibling's fill and keeps each property's own floor, so its ink is text at 4.5:1 (D89); a part declares `on` or `over`, never both, and with either it sits on the sibling's option of its own name where the sibling derives that option (D89). A group part with `mark: true` is a mark at 3:1 in every pair, over its own fill or, with no fill, over the ground; its fill is a mark over the ground only where its option draws no edge, with no edge answer or a `transparent` one, because a disc filled with the surface is told by its ring (D92), and that ring, the rest edge of such an option, pairs at 3:1 over the ground (D97). A group part with `mark: "ink"` pairs its ink and icon at 3:1 over its own fill or, with no fill, over the ground, and its fill pairs as it would without `mark`; `meters.track` declares it, because a range is a graphical object while its track fill is held at 3:1 over the page as any fill is. `mark: "ink"` is refused beside `on` or `over`, as `mark: true` is. `check:text-contrast` gates `level-presence` at 3:1 whatever property carries it, so `check:color-slots` refuses a slot at that level that pairs as text and a manifest that reads it outside a presence slot's chain. An option whose `channel` is `--arena-accent-<group>-<part>-{property}` is written by its `arena-accent-<option>` class in `frameworks/tailwind/vocabulary/arena-accent/Accent.family.json`, with the chain `scripts/generate/core/arena-cli/slots.ts:channelValue(catalogue, name, form)` gives, as `Emphasis.family.json` writes the `actions` channels (D95). Each option writes only the group channels its own slots declare, save `currentColor` on an option that derives no slot (`arena-accent-ink`), and one `--arena-<family>-fade-<state>` channel serves the one part of an option that derives that state.

`feedback` derives a banner, an icon on it and an action over it, each on the banner of its own option (D89). `ArenaToast.root` lists the accent options `plain` and `gold` beside its tone options: it reads the accent ones through `channels` in its base class and each tone option in its `optionBy` branch, so `optionBy` leaves the channelled options with no value, and the tone it states wins over the accent. A member reading an accent channel reads it bare, because the accent family's default block writes it on the member part's own `data-arena-part` hook, so the read holds only on an element carrying that hook. It draws its edge as a stripe, and its other three sides read `edge-surface` (D90). A pair is measured over the ground its group part names, and a member that declares `ground` is measured over the fill it paints on as well (D96): `ArenaToast.action` over `fill-surface-floating` beside the neutral banner fill, and a Spinner's arcs over every surface (`"ground"`) beside the track fill.

## A member part

A member part that reads a group is an object `{ to, options, states, channels?, defaults?, decision?, optionBy?, selectedBy?, disabledBy?, hoverBy?, onlyBy?, hoverIn?, fillAs?, edgeAs?, disabledModifier?, inkFrom?, properties?, inline?, ground?, groundPart?, toBy?, seriesBy?, wrappedBy? }`: `onlyBy` paints the member only where its axis holds (`ArenaFileInput.zone` paints its field where it is droppable) and `inkFrom` reads the member's text from another group part (`fields.value`); `optionBy` is one axis or a list of them (`ArenaInput.field` takes its option from `state` and from `readOnly`), an entry `{ axes: [a, b], values: { "<va>,<vb>": "<option>" } }` reads its option in the compound variant where both values hold (D40), and `properties` lists the properties of the group part the member paints (`ArenaCommandPalette.search` paints the edge alone); `inline` reads the member's chains in its own class instead of through a channel, as `ArenaCalendar.nav` reads the secondary option; `disabledBy` reads the disabled states inside that variant branch with no modifier, for a part that is no native control and never matches `disabled:` (`ArenaCheckbox.box`, D71); `channels` reads a property
through a channel of its own (`ArenaIconButton.root` reads its edge through `--arena-emphasis-icon-edge`), and a `defaults` entry is a
member default, which replaces the group default at the end of that member's chain while the slot is silent and needs the decision that
grants it (the `decision` field of `ArenaIconButton.root` names the one for its ghost edge); `check:color-slots` holds a default to one rest slot of the member's own part, on an option and a state it lists, answered with `transparent` or a role that slot accepts, and never the answer the group already gives; `scripts/generate/core/arena-cli/slots.ts:memberChain(catalogue, member, name)` writes that chain.

`hoverIn` reads the hover and pressed states inside that variant branch, under `hover:` and `active:`, so only a linked crumb or a reachable step hovers (`ArenaBreadcrumbs.crumb`, `ArenaStepper.trigger`). `edgeAs: "rule"` draws each edge slot as the inset rule `shadow-[inset_0_calc(var(--bw-strong)*-1)_0_<chain>]` in place of a border (`ArenaTabs.tab`). `edgeAs: "stripe"` draws each edge slot on the left border alone, as `border-l-[color:<chain>]`, and `check:color-slots` refuses an edge slot read on any other side. `fillAs: "stroke"` reads each fill slot as `stroke-[color:<chain>]` in place of `bg-[color:<chain>]`, for a fill an SVG draws as a stroke. `edgeAs: "fill"` reads each edge slot as `bg-[color:<chain>]`, for a rule drawn as a 1 px box, and `check:color-slots` refuses it on a member whose part paints a fill. `disabledModifier: "aria-disabled:"` reads the disabled and selected-disabled states, and their fade markers, under `aria-disabled:`, for a control that stays focusable while disabled (`ArenaBottomNav.item`). `ArenaAccordion.trigger` reads its disabled slots under `aria-disabled:` too and carries no fade, because there `aria-disabled` marks a panel locked open. `check:color-slots` refuses `hoverIn` on a member that reads no hover or pressed state, `disabledModifier` on one that reads no disabled state, and a member state its group part does not derive, since the member would read no slot for it.

`ground` names the fill role a member paints on, a `fill-*` or `overlay-*` role, or `"ground"` for the page, and `scripts/generate/core/arena-cli/slot-contrast.ts:slotReports(catalogue, answers, roles, colors, theme, levels)` measures each ink, icon and selected-edge pair of the slots the member reads over that fill, or over each surface for `"ground"`, through the part's own fill where the member reads one that is not opaque (an opaque one is the pair's ground already), and its line names the member (D96). `groundPart: "<part>"` names instead another part of the same member that paints a fill, and the same pairs are measured over the rest fill that part paints in each option it lists, composited over each surface where that fill is not opaque, so the line names that fill slot, and a fill that resolves to no palette colour is named by `scripts/generate/core/arena-cli/slot-contrast.ts:unresolvedPairs(catalogue, answers, roles, colors, levels)` as a pair never measured (D25): `ArenaAlert.title` and `ArenaAlert.message` sit on `ArenaAlert.root`, the banner of their tone. `toBy: { axis, values: { "<value>": "<group>.<part>" } }` reads another part, one that takes no option, of the member's own group, of the group of `to` or of `text`, inside that axis branch while the base class reads `to`, and the member counts as a reader of each part it names (D104, D107): `ArenaCalendar.dayNumber` reads `text.body` and, in its `today=true` branch, `data.today`. `seriesBy: "<axis>"` names an axis with a `hues` entry: inside its branches the member reads only `var(--arena-hue-…)`, outside them it reads only its slots or a keyword (`transparent`, `current`, `inherit`), each slot by its chain or through the channel its option declares, and it states no option while that axis holds a value. `wrappedBy: "--arena-<family>-{property}"` reads each slot as `var(--arena-<family>-<property>,<chain>)`, so a family that writes the channel wins, and `check:color-slots` holds the inner chain equal to the slot's (D98).

Beside the refusals above, `scripts/check/core/check-color-slots.ts:declarationProblems(d, roles)` refuses a member part written as a string other than `"series"` (D86), and for a member part an `edgeAs` other than `rule`, `stripe` or `fill`, and an edge drawn on a part that paints no edge; a `fillAs` other than `stroke` or on a part that paints no fill; `hoverIn` beside `hoverBy` or `onlyBy`; a `ground` that is neither `"ground"` nor a `fill-*` or `overlay-*` role, or on a member that reads no ink, icon or selected edge; a `groundPart` that names no part of the member, one that names a part reading no group or painting no fill, and one beside `ground`, since the two are one choice (D25); a `toBy` part outside the member's group, the group of `to` and `text`, one with options, `to` itself or one named twice; a `seriesBy` that names no axis, a `toBy` or `seriesBy` axis another branch key already uses, and a `toBy` axis its `seriesBy` names; and a `wrappedBy` that is not `--arena-<family>-{property}`. A member part reads each slot through its own channel, or, where the slot has none, as exactly `memberValue(catalogue, member, slot, 'tailwind')` under its state's modifier and axis branch, inside its `toBy` branch for a part `toBy` names and inside `var(--arena-<family>-<property>,…)` for `wrappedBy`.

A member part whose value is `"series"` paints the colour of the series or column it stands for, outside the slot tier: each colour it reads is `var(--arena-hue-…)` inside a branch of an axis with a `hues` entry, and `check:color-slots` refuses any other read (D75, D76).

Beside the series parts, two group parts are painted by the file their `paintedBy` names and no member reads them: `marks.solid` by `Mark.family.json` (D98) and `surfaces.skeleton` by `Animations.css`. Two member parts read roles directly: `ArenaToast.root` paints its floating fill and its three quiet sides with `fill-surface-floating` and `edge-surface` (D90), and `ArenaAvatar.status` cuts its ring out of the surface with the role `fill-surface` (the `border-surface` utility). Every other fill, ink, icon and edge a manifest part paints reads a slot, save the focus ring, which reads `edge-focus`.

A group part with no `rest`, no `selected` and no option derives no slot and names the properties and states a part may be answered for, as `surfaces.skeleton` above. A part that answers `rest` derives its slots: `overlays.readout` does, and the chart components map their tooltips onto it (D75, D88).

A member with `selectedBy` that reads `hover` or `pressed` reads `selected-hover` or `selected-pressed` too, and reads its rest hover in the unselected branch, the complement of a `true` or `false` value, so a hovered selected part never matches the rest hover (R4); `check:color-slots` refuses such a rest hover read in the base class, and a `selectedBy` value other than `true` or `false` on such a member. A `disabledBy` member that hovers or presses repeats each disabled and selected-disabled read under `hover:` and `active:` in the same branch, so a hovered disabled part keeps an answered disabled slot once its fade stops (D8); a hover read through `hoverBy` is no pointer hover and needs no repeat.

A group's `pressed` state derives from its rest, mixed toward the part's own ink at `mix-pressed`, on every pressable member of `menus`, `navigation` and `steps`: Menu, Accordion, Sheet, SideNav, TreeView, Pagination, BottomNav and `ArenaStepper.trigger`. A selected hover or pressed edge mixes the selected edge toward the selected ink at its fill's mix (D85), so the rule under a hovered tab moves with the wash beside it.
