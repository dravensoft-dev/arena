Identity document

The appearance of this product, written down before any of it is built, so that what gets built is what somebody decided rather than what an agent inferred. Every section below is a question Arena's style kernel asks. Answer it here, get the answer approved by whoever owns the product, and only then write arena.config.json and the style plugin: the file and the plugin are this document in the form a build reads.

Replace every dashed block. A block left as it is means the decision was never made, and a decision nobody made is one the build makes silently.

What is being built, who works in it, and on what.

The product, its register, and who is pointing at it.
