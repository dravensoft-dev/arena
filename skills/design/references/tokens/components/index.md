# Which roles answer a part of a component?

Read this when your style plugin answers a role a component reads. Such a role names a family option such as a size or an elevation, or answers one part of a component.

Your markup reads the roles on the [markup pages](../markup/index.md), and a role here answers a part only Arena's components draw. The `<option>` roles are in [`StylePlugins.md`](../../../../../contracts/design/StylePlugins.md#the-option-roles). A component role draws from the scale of the plugin role with its prefix, on the [plugin pages](../plugin/index.md). `pad-*` and `gap-*` read `sp`, and `timeline-dot` reads `sp` though no plugin role has its prefix. A `size-*` role reads the scale of the part its name ends in, `-gap` and `-pad-x` from `sp`. A `size-*` role takes a literal where no step fits, as most `size-*` lengths and every `grid-*`, `board-*`, `dialog-*` and `scroller-*` do. The `hue`, `accent`, `emphasis` and `elevation` roles answer as their type does. Three name groups read another scale, each in [`spacing.json`](../../../../../contracts/design/spacing.json):

- `size-*-avatar` and `size-*-face` read `avatar`.
- `size-*-logo-*` reads `logo`.
- `size-*-control-h` and every other `*-step` read `dz`.

`mix-hover` and `mix-pressed` read `blend` in [`effects.json`](../../../../../contracts/design/effects.json) and sit on [the states page](../plugin/states.md).

A group's colours sit in [`color-slots.md`](../../color-slots.md#the-groups).
