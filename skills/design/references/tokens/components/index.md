# Which roles answer a part of a component?

Read this when your style plugin answers a role that only a component reads, whether its name carries a family option such as a size, an elevation or an emphasis, or the role answers one part of one component.

A component role is read by the component itself, and no markup of yours reads one. The `<option>` roles are stated in [`contracts/design/StylePlugins.md`](../../../../../contracts/design/StylePlugins.md#the-option-roles), under "The option roles". A component role draws from the scale of the plugin role with its prefix, as the [plugin pages](../plugin/index.md) name: `pad-*` and `gap-*` from `sp`, for one. A `size-*` role reads the scale of the part its name ends in, `-gap` and `-pad-x` from `sp`, and takes a literal where no step fits, as most `size-*` lengths and every `grid-*`, `board-*`, `dialog-*` and `scroller-*` role do. The `hue`, `accent`, `emphasis` and `elevation` roles answer as their type does. Three groups of names read another scale, each in [`spacing.json`](../../../../../contracts/design/spacing.json):

- `size-*-avatar` and `size-*-face` read `avatar`.
- `size-*-logo-*` reads `logo`, the logo steps among them.
- `size-*-control-h` and every other `*-step` read `dz`.

The mix roles `mix-hover` and `mix-pressed` read `blend` in [`effects.json`](../../../../../contracts/design/effects.json), and they sit on [the states page](../plugin/states.md).
