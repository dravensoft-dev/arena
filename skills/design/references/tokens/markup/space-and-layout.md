# What do I pad, space, size and lay out my own markup with?

Read this when your own markup needs a gap, the padding of a control, a column or the clearance of a device's edge.

**`--sp-*` is the spacing scale**, and everything the rhythm classes do not cover reads it. A bare length is a bug, and this scale makes it unnecessary. [`contracts/design/Scales.md`](../../../../../contracts/design/Scales.md) says what each step is for.

**`--pad-safe-top`, `--pad-safe-right`, `--pad-safe-bottom` and `--pad-safe-left`** compose the
device's own insets with that scale, in
[`contracts/design/environment.css`](../../../../../contracts/design/environment.css). Use them on a
shell you draw around Arena, so a bar pinned to the bottom of a phone screen clears the home
indicator without you measuring one.

The values of the scale are on [the spacing page](../scales/spacing-radius-border.md).

**Space is composed from four reads.** Take the air inside a surface from `--pad-surface` and its head roles, and the air between its items from the `--gap-*` role of that grouping. A row's air is `px-row-x` and `py-row-y`, which follow the density of the screen. In CSS of your own, take the `calc` that the row of `--pad-row-x` or `--pad-row-y` gives, since a bare `var(--pad-row-x)` does not follow it. A control's air is `--pad-control-x` and `--pad-control-y`. The column and the gutter come from `--container-max` and `--gutter`.

Your markup and Arena's components both read the roles on this page. A role keeps its name until a major release, and its value is the style plugin's to change in any release.

| role | question | utility | combines with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `--aspect-media` | What width to height ratio does a media frame take? | none | none |
| `--container-max` | How wide does the content column of a page get? | `max-w-page` | none |
| `--fit-media` | How does an image fill the edges of its media frame? | none | none |
| `--gap-actions` | How far apart are the buttons in the action row of a dialog or sheet? | `gap-actions` | none |
| `--gap-control` | How far apart are the parts inside one control, such as an icon and its label? | `gap-control` | none |
| `--gap-inline` | How far apart are siblings that sit on one line, such as toolbar actions or chips? | `gap-inline` | none |
| `--gap-items` | How far apart are the members of a group, such as form fields or grid cards? | `gap-items` | none |
| `--gap-row` | How far apart are the parts of a row, such as an icon and its text? | `gap-row` | none |
| `--gutter` | How much air sits between the content column and the viewport edge? | `px-gutter` | none |
| `--pad-control-text-y` | How much room sits above and below a field or skip link sized by its text? | `py-control-text-y` | none |
| `--pad-control-x` | How much room does a button leave on its left and right? | `px-control-x` | none |
| `--pad-control-y` | How much room does a button leave above and below its content? | `py-control-y` | none |
| `--pad-row-indent` | How far does each level of nesting indent a row? | none | none |
| `--pad-row-x` | How much room does a list, feed or side nav row leave on its left and right? | `px-row-x` | `calc(var(--pad-row-x) * var(--dz-row-scale-x))` |

| `--pad-surface-head` | How much room does a surface leave under its head? | `pb-surface-head` | none |
| `--pad-surface-head-top` | How much room does a surface leave above its title? | `pt-surface-head-top` | none |
