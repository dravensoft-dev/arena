# What do I set my own text in?

Read this when your own markup sets a face, a weight, a size, a line height or a tracking.

A role names the face, the weight or the line height for one kind of text, and the scale holds the steps behind it. The steps are on [the type scale page](../scales/type-scale.md).

**Text is composed from a face, a weight, a case, a tracking and a step.** Take the `--ff-*`, `--fw-*`, `--tt-*` and `--track-*` roles of the kind of text you draw. Add the `--step-title-*` role when it is a title, from the hero step to the surface step, or `--step-eyebrow` or `--step-label` for those two kinds. `--lh-*` and `--measure-prose` set the leading and the width of a column.
