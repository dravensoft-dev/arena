# What does a state draw, and which selector shows it?

Read this when your own markup shows a state, such as hover, keyboard focus, selection, a pressed toggle, a disabled control or an invalid field.

**Thirteen roles answer the states of a control, and the second table says which attribute or pseudo-class shows each one.** `--fill-hover` covers a cell as well as a row. Focus, selection and invalidity reach utilities through `ring-edge-focus`, `outline-edge-focus`, `bg-selected`, `text-ink-selected`, `border-edge-selected`, `border-edge-invalid` and `text-ink-invalid`. The selected or current item of a navigation component takes `border-edge-tab-selected`, and any other selected item takes `border-edge-selected`. The focus width, the focus gap and the disabled fade have no key of their own. The three are read through arbitrary forms: `outline-[length:var(--bw-focus)]` or `ring-[length:var(--bw-focus)]`, `outline-offset-[var(--gap-focus)]` or `ring-offset-[var(--gap-focus)]`, and `opacity-[var(--fade-disabled)]`.

## A toggle that shows every state

A pressed toggle washes with `--fill-selected` and reads `--ink-selected`, a keyboard user sees `--edge-focus` at `--bw-focus` and `--gap-focus`, and a disabled one keeps `--fade-disabled` of its opacity. An invalid field takes `--edge-invalid` on its edge only while `aria-invalid` is true and `--ink-invalid` on its message.

```html
<button type="button" aria-pressed="false"
  class="aria-pressed:bg-selected/(--level-accent-soft-primary) aria-pressed:text-ink-selected focus-visible:outline-edge-focus focus-visible:outline-[length:var(--bw-focus)] focus-visible:outline-offset-[var(--gap-focus)] disabled:opacity-[var(--fade-disabled)]">
  Notify me
</button>

<label for="email">Email address</label>
<input id="email" aria-invalid="true" aria-describedby="email-error" class="border border-edge-field aria-[invalid=true]:border-edge-invalid" />
<p id="email-error" class="text-ink-invalid">Enter an address such as name@example.com.</p>
```

A nested style plugin's answer reaches `--edge-focus` and the other roles, and your markup reads those roles, never `--focus-ring`. Under a nested plugin class `--edge-focus` follows the subtree's answer, while `--focus-ring` keeps the root's.
