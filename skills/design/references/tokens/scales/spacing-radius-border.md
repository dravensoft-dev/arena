# Which spacing, radius, border, shadow and breakpoint values exist?

Read this when markup of yours needs a length, a corner, a border width, a shadow or a breakpoint, and the role pages name none.

**`--sp-*`** is the spacing scale. **`--r-*`** is the corner radius, and `--r-pill` is the fully rounded end. **`--bw`** and **`--bw-*`** are border widths. **`--shadow-*`** is the depth a surface casts. **`--bp-*`** is the breakpoint widths.

**A number counts steps of the 4px grid.** `p-7` or `gap-1.5` is its number times `--sp-1`. A whole number lands on the 4px grid, named step or not. A half step such as `gap-1.5` lands on the 2px half line, as Arena's own components do. The named steps are the ones a role or a page rhythm reads. Where a role answers the question, write the role, such as `--gap-items` between the members of a group or `--pad-surface` inside a card. A style plugin can change a role, and a number stays where you put it.

`*-` stands for any spacing utility, such as `h-` or `py-`. The sheet sets the spacing base to `--sp-1`, so a spacing utility resolves as its number times that step. The three `--rhythm-*` steps are the air between two components, and [`style.md`](../../style.md#the-air-between-two-components) says which class carries each. A row marked `none` has no utility named for its token, so read it with `var()`. Where the sheet resets a Tailwind default scale (radius and shadow), a utility of that default, such as `shadow-md`, emits nothing. `arena audit` reports it as `dead-utility` when your project resolves `tailwindcss`. A breakpoint row names the variant that reads it, and Tailwind's other default widths, such as `xl:`, stop resolving, and the audit reports them the same way.
