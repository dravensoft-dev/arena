# Colouring a group of components

Read this when your style plugin gives a group of components a colour of its own, or gives one state of a part a colour of its own. [`style-kernel.md`](./style-kernel.md) holds the roles and the loop.

A colour slot is one coloured part of one group of components, in one option, one property and one state. Your style plugin answers a slot with a colour role, in `plugin.tokens.json` beside the roles. **An answer moves every component that paints that part of the group.** Two components of one group move together, and a group is the smallest thing a slot colours.

A slot takes a role and never a palette colour. The role says which of your colours, and the slot says where it lands. A slot you leave silent paints what this page lists, so a plugin answers only the slots it changes.

## The common case first

**Keep the brand on the primary action and take it off the selection.** A screen reads its brand where its one main action is. A checked box, a switch that is on and the current navigation item read quieter when they do not repeat it. These answers do that:

The primary action needs no answer. `actions-primary-root-fill-rest` reads `emphasis-primary-fill`, which your plugin already answers with the brand. The first answer fills a checked box, a selected radio, a switch that is on and a slider's range with `fill-mark`. The second draws the check and the dot in `ink-on-mark`. The third sets the knob of a switch and a slider on the page colour, and the fourth draws the glyph on a switch's knob in the body ink. These components move together because each paints a part of the `choices` group. Four other groups quiet the navigation selection. The current or selected item of a side nav, a bottom nav, a pagination and a tree view takes a `fill-marker` wash instead of the primary one. The selected tab has no fill to wash. The selected edge is `edge-mark` in both polarities. That edge shows only where the item draws one, which is the rule under the selected tab and the border of the current page in a pagination. A side nav, a bottom nav and a tree view item draw no border. The count on the current row of a side nav and every count on a bottom nav take the quiet `fill-marker` with the body ink. The dot on a collapsed side nav rail takes `fill-marker`.

## How a slot is named

A slot is named `<group>-<option>-<part>-<property>-<state>`, and a part with no options leaves the option out: `actions-primary-root-fill-hover`, `actions-dismiss-ink-hover`. The property is `fill`, `ink`, `edge` or `icon`. The state is `rest`, `hover`, `pressed`, `selected`, `selected-hover`, `selected-pressed`, `disabled` or `selected-disabled`, and a part answers only the states its row below lists.

Your package's `arena.tokens.json` carries the catalogue under `slots`, and its own `slots` map names every slot. A `rest` or `selected` slot carries the role it answers as its `default`. Every icon slot, a disabled one included, carries a `default` naming its part's ink slot in the same state. A silent `hover` or `pressed` state, and its `selected` form, carries a `derive` naming the slot it follows, unless an exception answers it with a role. Any other disabled slot carries neither.

## What a slot takes

Each property takes the roles below and nothing else. A `*` stands for any word, so `fill-*` takes `fill-page` and `fill-marker`. `arena build` refuses an answer outside the list.

| property | a slot of it takes |
| --- | --- |
| `fill` | `emphasis-*-fill`, `emphasis-*-fill-hover`, `fill-*`, `hue-*-fill-strong`, `hue-*-fill-soft`, `accent-*-fill-soft`, `overlay-*`, `transparent` |
| `ink` | `emphasis-*-ink`, `ink-*`, `hue-*-ink`, `hue-*-on-ink`, `accent-*-ink`, `accent-*-quiet-ink` |
| `edge` | `emphasis-*-edge`, `emphasis-*-icon-edge`, `edge-*`, `hue-*-edge`, `accent-*-edge`, `accent-*-side-edge`, `transparent` |

## What a silent slot paints

A silent slot follows the slot of its own part that you did answer, so answering a `rest` slot moves its silent states with it.

- **`rest`** paints the role in the part's row below.
- **`hover`** paints the rest answer, except where a section below lists a role of its own under the pointer.
- **`pressed`** mixes the rest fill toward the part's own ink by `mix-pressed`. The ink, the edge and the icon keep their rest answer.
- **`selected`** paints the role in the part's selected column below.
- **`selected-hover`** and **`selected-pressed`** mix the selected fill and edge toward the selected ink by `mix-hover` and `mix-pressed`.
- **`disabled`** and **`selected-disabled`** keep the part's colours and fade it by `fade-disabled`. Answer one and the whole part stops fading in that state, so its other colours paint at full strength.
- **`icon`** paints the ink of the same part and state, in every state, unless you answer it.

A slot that defaults to a role follows your answer to that role. Answer `hue-danger-ink` and every silent slot whose default is `hue-danger-ink` moves, in every group.

## What arena check measures

`arena check` measures the slot pairs in every palette, in the `rest`, `hover`, `pressed`, `selected`, `selected-hover` and `selected-pressed` states:

- each ink, icon and fill over the fill of the part it sits on, and a part with no fill over the ground.
- each selected edge over the fill of its part.
- a resting edge only on a mark, over the ground, so look at an edge you answer.
- the focus ring over the fill of a part whose ring sits on that fill, in each state the fill paints. The parts measured so are a navigation item over its selected fill, the skip link over its fill and a table header over its fill. A ring that sits on the ground is measured over the page, surface, field and track fills instead.
- ArenaAlert's title and message over the banner of each tone, composited over each surface where the banner is translucent.
- a part a component sets on a surface of its own over that surface, such as a chart's tooltip label over the raised surface.
- a part a component paints with a default of its own, as the component paints it.

A fill with nothing under it is not a pair. A pair falls short under 4.5:1 for text, and under 3:1 for a fill, an icon, an edge or a mark. A `disabled` or `selected-disabled` slot is never measured. The build reports instead a `disabled` answer that draws the part's rest colours. The build reports a `selected-disabled` one that draws its selected colours in the same way. Either control would not be told from an enabled one.

`arena check` measures the root plugin and every later one. A later plugin is measured in every palette, its answers over the root's. A line it prints starts with `<palette>, style plugin <name>:` and appears only when the root's measurement in that palette did not print the same line. Each is a report, `arena build` prints only their count, and `arena check --strict=contrast` holds them. A runtime palette measures the same pairs through the root plugin, in `arenaCheckPalette` as in `arenaRuntimePalette`.

## A slot and plugin.css

**A `plugin.css` rule wins over the slot of the part it styles.** The sheet sits in a cascade layer after the component rules that read the slots. A declaration on a part's property therefore paints over that part's slot in every state the rule selects. A rule with no state selector paints every state, hover and selected included. Answer a slot for a colour that is a state of the part, and keep `plugin.css` for what no slot carries. `arena audit` reports a rule that repeats its slot as `restated`.

## What no slot reaches

- **A chart's series and heat steps** read `cat-1` to `cat-8` and `heat-1` to `heat-5` from your palette in `arena.config.json`. A chart's legend swatch follows its series.
- **The keyboard focus ring** is the role `edge-focus` on every part. No slot takes it.
- **A solid badge and the loading shimmer** paint outside the slot tier. A solid badge reads the hue roles.
- **How far a colour is held back** is a level, not a colour, and no slot answer moves it.
- **A toast's own surface** reads roles directly: `ArenaToast.root` paints its floating fill with `fill-surface-floating` and its three quiet sides with `edge-surface`, so answer those roles.
- **An avatar's status ring** is cut out of the surface: `ArenaAvatar.status` paints its ring with the role `fill-surface`, so the ring follows the surface you answer.
