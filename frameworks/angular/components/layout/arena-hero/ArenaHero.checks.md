# ArenaHero by hand (Angular)

Serve the tree with `bun run demos`, open `/frameworks/angular/components/layout/arena-hero/ArenaHero.demo.generated.html`, and check in a real browser:

- Narrow the window from wide: the split falls to one column at one step and never overflows.
- Under `bleed`, the words stay readable over the wash in both themes.
- Under `arena-align-center`, the whole column centres, including the actions row.
- With a screen reader running, the title is announced as a level one and the hero announces no landmark of its own.
