# ArenaMeter by hand (Angular)

Serve the tree with `bun run demos`, open `/frameworks/angular/components/feedback/arena-meter/ArenaMeter.demo.generated.html`, and check in a real browser:

- The fill matches the track at `arena-size-sm`, `md` and `lg`.
- Read Good, Fair and Poor in both themes, each word beside its reading.
- With a screen reader, the name, the reading and the standing are spoken once, and nothing is spoken on load.
