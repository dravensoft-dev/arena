# ArenaSlider by hand (Angular)

Serve the tree with `bun run demos`, open `/frameworks/angular/components/forms/arena-slider/ArenaSlider.demo.generated.html`, and check in a real browser.

The arrows, Home, End and the page keys. A drag and a press on the rail, with a mouse and with a finger. The focus ring on a focused thumb. The thumb target under `arena-comfortable` and at 390px. A vertical slider in a column of fixed height. A range whose thumbs meet.
